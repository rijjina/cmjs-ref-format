# CMJS (Chiang Mai Journal of Science) Style & Reference Rules

## 1. Manuscript In-Text Citations
* Numerical system enclosed in square brackets: `[1]`, `[1, 2]`, `[3-5]`, `[5, 11, 14-16]`.
* Numbered strictly in the order of first appearance in the main text.
* Must not use author-year format in the text (e.g., do NOT use `Smith et al. (2020)`).

## 2. CMJS Reference List Rules (Vancouver Variant)
* **Font & Size:** Calibri 10pt.
* **Numerical Format:** `[1]`, `[2]` with a tab or space following the bracket.
* **Author Format:** `Surname Initials` (e.g., `Kananthai A.`, `Phukaoluan A. and Khantachawana A.`).
  * If more than 6 authors, list the first 6 followed by `et al.`.
* **ARTICLE TITLES:** **CMJS DOES NOT INCLUDE ARTICLE TITLES FOR JOURNAL PAPERS.**
  * *Standard Journal Format:*  
    `[No.] Author(s), Abbrev. Journal Title, Year; Volume(Issue): Pages. DOI 10.xxxx/yyyy`
  * *Example:*  
    `[1] Kananthai A., J. Comp. Appl. Math., 1997; 84: 101-106. DOI 10.1007/s11274-013-1451-9`
* **Journal Names:** Abbreviated according to standard ISO-4 rules with trailing periods (e.g., `J. Mater. Sci.`, `Appl. Energy`, `Mater. Sci. Eng. A`).
* **Volume & Issue:** Year; Volume(Issue): Page-Page.
* **DOI Format:** `DOI 10.xxxx/yyyy` (strip `https://doi.org/`).
* **Books:** Author(s), Book Title, Edition, Publisher, City, Year: Pages.
